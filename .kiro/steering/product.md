# Product Steering — Ghost Pomodoro

## Vision
Procrastination is scary. Ghost Pomodoro makes the scary part the fun part:
your to-do list is a graveyard of cursed souls, and the only way to free them
is to focus. The ghost is not a mascot slapped on a timer — it watches you,
judges you, cheers for you, and haunts you when you quit early.

## Who it is for
- Students (like ZF at UNED) with essays, readings, and deadlines that feel cursed.
- Anyone who has tried a plain Pomodoro app and bounced off it in a week.
- Kiroween judges, who should understand the app in 10 seconds and be able
  to use it in one click — no signup, no tutorial, no setup.

## What it must feel like
- **Instant:** open the page, see the blood moon, press Start. Two starter
  tasks are already cursed so the first run needs zero typing.
- **Playful, not punishing:** a haunt is a joke with a shake and a sting, not
  a guilt trip. Souls and XP always move forward.
- **Haunted, not unreadable:** the spooky UI must never hurt legibility.
  Big timer, high-contrast buttons, readable body font. If a judge cannot
  read it, the costume has failed.

## Costume Contest angle
The category asks for any app with a haunting UI that is polished and
unforgettable, where spooky design enhances function. Here it does:
- The progress ring is a blood moon that drains as focus drains the curse.
- Fog thickens when you abandon a focus — the UI literally reacts to behaviour.
- The ghost's mood is the app's status indicator.
- Candles, cobwebs, and floating ghosts set the scene without adding clicks.

## Non-goals
- No accounts, no backend, no sync, no social features.
- No AI generation inside the app (the Kiro story is about how it was built).
- No paywall, no ads, no tracking.
- No second app. One polished page beats two half-finished ones.

## Success looks like
- A judge opens the link, starts a 25-second demo focus, watches a task gain
  a Pomodoro, frees a soul, and smiles.
- The demo video shows a full cycle in under 3 minutes.
- The submission reads as a real product, not a demo prop.
