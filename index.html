    🕯️ How to play

    1. Curse a task — add it with a skull level and Pomodoro estimate.
    2. Click a task to select it, then press Start Focus.
    3. Finish the focus — Boo cheers, you earn souls and XP, the task gains a Pomodoro.
    4. Free the soul — when its Pomodoros are done, the task is freed. Abandon a focus early and Boo haunts you instead.
